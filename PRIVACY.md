# CameraMan Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 22, 2026_

CameraMan shows your camera in a floating window on your Mac. That is all it does with your camera.

## What CameraMan collects

Nothing. CameraMan:

- does not record, save or upload video or images;
- does not send any data over the network;
- has no account, analytics, advertising or tracking;
- does not share anything with third parties.

The camera image is processed on your Mac, in memory, only to draw the preview, and is discarded frame by frame.

## Data stored on your Mac

Your settings (chosen camera, shape, size, colors and so on) are saved on your Mac in the app's own storage, so they
are there the next time you open it. They never leave your Mac. Deleting the app removes them.

## Camera permission

macOS asks for your permission the first time CameraMan uses the camera. You can turn it off at any time in
System Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera.
