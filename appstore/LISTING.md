## Description (4000)

CameraMan puts your camera in a small floating window that stays on top of everything else on your screen. Perfect for
screen recordings, tutorials, product demos, live classes and presentations: your audience sees your screen and your
face at the same time.

SHAPES
• Circle, organic, square, vertical 9:16 and horizontal 16:9
• Organic shapes with smooth, natural outlines — pick a preset, shuffle a new one, or let it gently breathe
• Soft edge: fade yourself into the screen instead of a hard cutout
• Adjustable corner radius
• Press O to cycle through shapes

FRAMING
• Move the image inside the shape with the arrow keys
• Zoom in and out with + and −
• Mirror horizontally or flip vertically

LOOK
• Brightness, contrast and saturation, applied live
• Works with macOS video effects: Portrait blur, Studio Light, backgrounds
• Border in any color or a two-color gradient
• Soft shadow around the shape

MADE FOR RECORDING
• Global shortcuts: show or hide yourself (⌃⌥⌘C) and switch size (⌃⌥⌘S) without leaving the app you are recording
• Follows you to every desktop and over full-screen apps
• The camera turns off completely while hidden
• Sharp image with almost no CPU use, so your recorder gets the power

WINDOW
• Five sizes, from a small bubble to full screen
• Snap to any corner of the screen; each size remembers its own place
• Space bar switches between your two favourite sizes
• Drag it anywhere

MORE
• Switch between cameras, including iPhone Continuity Camera
• Menu bar icon for quick access, or run it from the menu bar only
• Open at login
• Settings window with search

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
CameraMan never records, saves or uploads anything. No account, no analytics, no network access. Your video stays on
your Mac.

## Notes for App Review

CameraMan shows the Mac's camera in a floating, always-on-top window for use during screen recordings and
presentations. On first launch macOS asks for camera access. Control+Option+Command+C shows and hides the camera
from any app. Settings are under CameraMan → Settings (⌘,) or the
viewfinder icon in the menu bar. Keyboard shortcuts work while the camera window is focused (listed in Settings →
Shortcuts). No account or sign-in is needed.
